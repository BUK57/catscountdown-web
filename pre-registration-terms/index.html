Pre-Registration Reward Terms

Last updated: May 2026

1. The Reward

Users who pre-registered for Cats Countdown on Google Play before its launch will receive the exclusive Gold app icon as a free in-app reward. This icon is permanently unlocked in your copy of the app and cannot be earned any other way.

2. Eligibility

To receive the Gold icon you must have:

  • Pre-registered for Cats Countdown on the Google Play Store before the app's public launch, and
  • Installed the app using the same Google account you used to pre-register

The reward is delivered automatically by Google Play when you first install the app. No code or manual redemption is required.

3. How It Works

When you install the app after pre-registering, Google Play automatically grants the Gold icon to your account. The app detects this grant on first launch and unlocks the icon for you. If the icon does not appear, please ensure you are signed in to the same Google account you used to pre-register, then close and reopen the app.

4. Exclusivity

The Gold icon is a limited, exclusive reward for pre-registrants only. It will not be made available for purchase or through any other means after the pre-registration period has ended.

5. No Monetary Value

The Gold icon has no monetary value and cannot be transferred, sold, exchanged, or redeemed for cash or any other benefit. It is a cosmetic feature tied to your Google Play account.

6. Changes and Availability

We reserve the right to modify or discontinue the pre-registration reward programme at any time. If you have already received the Gold icon, it will remain available to you within the app for as long as the app is supported.

7. Contact

If you pre-registered but have not received your Gold icon, or if you have any other questions, please contact us:

  • Email: support@catscountdown.com
  • Website: catscountdown.com
