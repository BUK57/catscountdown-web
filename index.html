About Cats Countdown

A cute cat countdown app & widget to track your birthdays, vacations & events!

Looking for a beautiful, cozy way to track your upcoming events? Meet Cats Countdown—the ultimate event timer and days until the counter that brings a smile to your face every time you check the clock!

Whether you need a vacation countdown, a wedding tracker, a birthday reminder, or a simple tool to count down the days until Christmas, our hand-illustrated feline friends are here to help you celebrate. Every countdown card comes to life with unique cat artwork and warm, aesthetic color themes.

The app is built with love by a small independent family (two humans, two cats and a dog).
